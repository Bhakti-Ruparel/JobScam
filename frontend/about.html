About Our Project

Learn how we're fighting internship scams with AI technology

🎯 Our Mission

We're on a mission to protect students and young professionals from falling victim to fake internship scams. Every year, thousands of job seekers lose money and personal information to fraudulent job postings. Our AI-powered platform helps identify these scams before they cause harm.

🤖 The Technology

Machine Learning Model

Trained on 27,566 job postings using Logistic Regression and Sentence Transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) to create embeddings that capture semantic meaning.

Natural Language Processing

Analyzes text for urgency indicators, emotional manipulation, grammar patterns, and suspicious phrases commonly found in scam postings.

Multi-Factor Analysis

Combines ML predictions with rule-based checks on websites, LinkedIn profiles, and screenshot analysis for comprehensive detection.

Real-Time Processing

FastAPI backend provides instant analysis with detailed breakdowns of risk factors and confidence scores.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool 100% accurate?

While our model achieves 95%+ accuracy, no automated system is perfect. Use it as a helpful tool alongside your own judgment and research.

What data do you collect?

We only analyze the information you provide (job text, URLs). We don't store personal information or track users.

Can I use this for any job posting?

Yes! While optimized for internships, it works for any job posting analysis.

What if I find a scam?

Report it to local authorities, the platform where you found it, and warn others in your network.

Is this service free?

Yes, completely free! Our goal is to protect as many job seekers as possible.
