Common Red Flags

💰

Upfront Payment

Asking for registration fees, security deposits, or training costs

⏰

Urgent Pressure

"Limited slots", "Apply immediately", "Offer expires soon"

🎯

Too Good to Be True

Unrealistic salaries, no experience required, guaranteed positions

📱

WhatsApp Only

Communication only through WhatsApp or personal messaging apps

❌

No Interview

Instant offers without proper interview or verification process

🏢

Vague Details

No clear company information, address, or official website
